Biosolutions in
everyday life.

A clean shirt, a glass of juice and the nutrition in animal feed all begin with materials and processes. See where enzymes can help and what a useful biosolution needs to achieve.

Enzymes at work
in the wash.

Protease helps break down protein-based stains such as milk on a shirt. The enzyme also needs to work with the detergent formulation and washing conditions. Those details matter when evaluating cleaning performance and the resources used in a wash.

Explore detergent applications→

How fruit
becomes juice.

Making good use of fruit starts with understanding the process. Our juice applications focus on extraction and clarification, where the enzyme's role depends on the fruit and the stage of production.

Explore juice applications→

Getting nutrition
from feed.

Animal nutrition depends on what an animal can use from its feed. We study how enzymes act on feed ingredients and how processing affects their activity, with better use of feed resources as the aim.

Research shaped by
real-world needs.

New products. New technology. New applications. New strains.

Useful biosolutions take shape through enzyme products, production methods, application research and microbial strains. Our four development priorities connect the biological starting point with real industrial requirements.

Fit the preparation to the formulation.

Our detergent range includes liquid and granular preparations and multi-enzyme options. Granules are used in powder detergents, while liquid preparations are used in liquid systems. We discuss product format and intended use when considering a suitable reference or a custom blend.

Explore formulation support

Give the science a production route.

SUNSON’s production background includes submerged fermentation and concentration using ultrafiltration membranes. Fermentation, separation, concentration and formulation give preparation development an industrial route. Explore the manufacturing information to understand the methods and the sites behind this work.

Explore manufacturing and quality

Let the application shape the question.

Raw materials, formulation conditions and intended use shape what needs to be investigated. Application research keeps these practical questions connected to enzyme selection and the discussion of a suitable preparation.

We provide sample storage and use guidance, then record observations, use levels and unexpected behaviour. If a trial falls short, the discussion returns to process conditions and parameters to consider an adjustment or another product reference.

See application testing support

Research begins with the microorganism.

SUNSON’s strain work includes screening and breeding enzyme-producing microorganisms. A collection of microbial strains provides resources for further research. Application questions sit alongside this work, keeping the biological source connected to what a preparation will be asked to do.

Two research centers connect the biological starting point with the work a preparation is intended to do.

Strain research center

Our microbial research includes strain screening and breeding. Our strain research center is supported by a joint laboratory focused on industrial microbiology.

Application research center

Resources for microbial research.

SUNSON’s research includes a collection of enzyme-producing microbial strains and gene-sequence submissions to GenBank. These resources form part of our work with the biological sources behind enzyme preparations.

Contributing to technical standards.

SUNSON has participated in drafting Chinese national standards on enzyme classification, quality requirements and activity testing. This work contributes to the technical definitions used to describe and assess enzyme preparations.

Read about standards participation

Enzyme manufacturing at SUNSON.

SUNSON began in 1996 and has manufacturing operations in Yinchuan and Cangzhou. Our team can arrange a site visit or video factory discussion focused on the location relevant to your product.

We sample and test products after production, then provide a certificate of analysis for the batch. Certificates for the Cangzhou site include ISO 9001, ISO 22000 and FAMI-QS. Check the named entity, scope and validity period on each document.
