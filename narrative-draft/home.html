A useful preparation begins with understanding both its biological source and the work it will be asked to do. Two research centers bring these perspectives together at SUNSON.

Application research center

Application research considers the material, operating conditions and intended use. Together, these perspectives frame the questions behind development.

Progress comes
from connected thinking.

New products. New technology. New applications. New strains.

A biological source, a preparation, a production method and a practical use each bring their own questions. Our continuing work connects these four directions.

Make function usable.

The way a preparation is handled belongs in the development discussion. Our detergent range includes liquid and granular forms, with custom product discussions that can include multi-enzyme blends. These are concrete ways to consider function alongside formulation and use.

Explore formulation support

Give the science a production route.

SUNSON’s manufacturing background includes submerged fermentation and membrane concentration. Along with separation and formulation, these methods provide a route from biological production to an enzyme preparation. The manufacturing story explains the stages and sites behind this work.

Explore manufacturing and quality

Bring observations back into the work.

Storage and use information accompanies a sample. Trial observations, use levels and unexpected behaviour give the technical discussion a practical record. When a trial does not meet expectations, our team reviews the conditions and parameters to discuss an adjustment or another preparation.

Keep investigating the biological source.

Research into enzyme-producing microorganisms connects the biological source with the possibilities for a preparation. Strain screening and breeding sit alongside application research, so development considers both what biology can do and where it may be useful.

Progress needs
a shared language.

SUNSON has participated in drafting Chinese national standards covering enzyme classification, quality requirements and activity testing. This contribution reaches beyond an individual preparation to the way technical work is described and compared.

From scientific possibility
to everyday production.

SUNSON began in 1996. Our manufacturing operations include Yinchuan and Cangzhou. Our order workflow includes sampling and testing after production, followed by a certificate of analysis for the relevant batch.

Certification documents for the Cangzhou site include ISO 9001, ISO 22000 and FAMI-QS, each with its own named entity, scope and certificate period. Site visits or video factory discussions can be arranged through our team to explore the operations and applicable documents.
