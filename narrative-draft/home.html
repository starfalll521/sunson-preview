Define what the trial will measure.

Start with the raw material, formulation or process step, then define the result you want to assess. Those details guide enzyme selection and the questions to investigate in an application trial.

We provide sample storage and use guidance and review observations, use levels and unexpected behaviour. If a trial falls short, we revisit the process conditions and discuss an adjustment or another product reference.

Research begins with the microorganism.

SUNSON’s strain work includes screening and breeding enzyme-producing microorganisms. A collection of microbial strains provides resources for further research. We also study how preparations from these biological sources could be used in practice.

Enzyme manufacturing at SUNSON.

Founded in 1996. We make enzymes in Yinchuan and Cangzhou, on 120,000 m² of site area. We can arrange a visit, in person or by video, to the site that makes your product.

After production, we sample and test products and provide a certificate of analysis (COA) for the batch. The Cangzhou site’s certificates include ISO 9001, ISO 22000 and FAMI-QS. Check the certificate holder, scope and validity period on each document.
