The research behind the everyday

A useful preparation begins with understanding both its biological source and the work it will be asked to do. Two research centers bring these perspectives together at SUNSON.

Strain research center

Our microbial research includes strain screening and breeding. Our strain research center is supported by a joint laboratory focused on industrial microbiology.

Application research center

Application research considers the material, operating conditions and intended use. Together, these perspectives frame the questions behind development.

The manufacturing
behind the preparation.

SUNSON began in 1996. Our manufacturing operations include Yinchuan and Cangzhou, connecting preparation development with production. Site visits or video factory discussions can be arranged through our team to explore the relevant site and its work.

Our workflow includes sampling and testing after production, followed by a certificate of analysis for the relevant batch. Certification documents for the Cangzhou site include ISO 9001, ISO 22000 and FAMI-QS, each with a named entity, scope and certificate period.
