Questions about distribution enquiries

Can I enquire about several industries?

List the product categories and the applications your customers need to evaluate. The relevant information can be reviewed for each category.

Do I need to provide customer names?

An initial enquiry can describe industries and applications. Discuss any further information-sharing arrangements as the cooperation review progresses.

Can I discuss a market I plan to enter?

Describe the market, intended customer industries and proposed approach. Product availability, documentation and cooperation requirements will need review.

Does an enquiry grant exclusive distribution rights?

Appointment and territory rights require separate agreement. Submitting this form does not grant exclusivity or permission to use SUNSON branding.

Can I request documents and samples for downstream customers?

Identify the product, intended use and customer evaluation requirements. Document-sharing and sample arrangements will be reviewed for the request.
