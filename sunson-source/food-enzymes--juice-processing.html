Enzymes for juice clarification and filtration

Start with the cause of haze and the juice style you want to produce. Suspended fruit particles, residual pectin and other haze-forming components raise different questions; an enzyme treatment does not replace that diagnosis.

For pectin-related treatment, review the selected preparation against the material and treatment stage. Record pH, temperature, treatment time, the separation method and any changes in raw material with the reference results.

Compare clarity or turbidity using a stated method and observation time. Assess filtration under comparable conditions. A clearer sample does not establish a higher juice yield: recovered juice quantity needs its own input and output boundary.
