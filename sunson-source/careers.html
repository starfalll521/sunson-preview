Fields of work

A biological question.
A practical setting.

Our strain research centre and application research centre bring two perspectives to enzyme development. Manufacturing and quality work connect the preparation with the information needed for its intended use.

01Strain research

Investigating enzyme-producing microorganisms, including screening and breeding. SUNSON’s research background includes a microbial strain collection and gene-sequence submissions to GenBank.

Research at SUNSON ↗
02Application research

Studying an enzyme with the material, process or formulation in which it will be used. Our current application information spans animal feed, juice processing and detergents.

Explore enzyme applications ↗
03Manufacturing and quality

Production operations in Yinchuan and Cangzhou form part of SUNSON’s manufacturing background. Fermentation, separation, concentration, formulation and product records connect laboratory understanding with an enzyme preparation.
