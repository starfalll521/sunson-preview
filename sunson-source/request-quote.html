Questions about quotation requests

Can I request an estimate before selecting a product?

Describe the application and purchase stage. The product reference and specification may need clarification before a price can be offered.

Is there a standard minimum order quantity?

Include the quantity you are considering. Minimum order and packaging requirements will be confirmed for the selected product and supply arrangement.

Can I ask for more than one product?

List each product with its quantity and any specific requirements. If the references are undecided, describe the intended application for each.

Can I use my nominated freight forwarder?

Include that preference and the proposed delivery arrangement. The applicable shipping terms can be reviewed during the quotation discussion.

Does submitting this form place an order?

The form starts a quotation enquiry. Product specifications and commercial arrangements need to be confirmed before an order proceeds.
