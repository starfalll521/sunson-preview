Current implementation notes

Confirmed inputs in use: company identity and contacts, supplied product materials and the agreed application scope.

Implementation: production domain, enquiry delivery and actual data-processing configuration remain part of the launch work. Forms in this preview do not submit.

Media: concept scenes and packaging positions are layout aids; company media replaces them at the final asset stage.

About this preview

This local preview represents SUNSON, Sunson Industry Group Co., Ltd. Technical contact: applications@sunsonzymes.com. This page describes the preview implementation; it is not the final production privacy policy.

Information entered in forms

The preview includes name, email, enquiry and optional project fields. Submission is disabled. The application does not send or save information entered into these forms. A product selected on another page may be carried in the URL and displayed in the form.

Media and external resources

Concept images are served locally. The reference-derived font definitions load font files from an external asset host. The SUNSON preview does not load the reference website’s analytics scripts, marketing form service or newsletter tracking.

Before the production website launches

The production policy must describe the actual hosting, form recipients, processors, retention and applicable privacy arrangements. These operational choices have not been implemented in this local preview. Existing company identity and contact materials are already available.
