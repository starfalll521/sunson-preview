Plan and interpret an enzyme application trial

Prepare a useful comparison, record the conditions and interpret the result against the original objective. This guide brings together practical principles from SUNSON’s feed, juice-processing and detergent application content.

Plan your sample comparison
On this page

Define the question and the comparison

Write down the production or formulation question before starting a trial. Identify the material, enzyme reference, treatment stage and result you want to measure.

Keep a clear reference treatment. Record the ingredient or process change being investigated and keep the other relevant conditions comparable. If several things change together, retain that information when interpreting the result.

Record the conditions that explain the result

Read the result against the original objective

Compare like-for-like measurements using the same units and a clearly defined calculation. State the input and output boundaries when reporting juice yield, and the sampling interval when reporting retained enzyme activity.

Keep observations, measured results, sample identity and any change in conditions together. Share that record with our application team when discussing the next step.
