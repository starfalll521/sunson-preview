Enzyme manufacturing and quality

SUNSON’s production background spans facilities in Yinchuan and Cangzhou and capabilities in fermentation, separation, concentration and formulation. Supplier review connects this manufacturing background with the selected product.

From fermentation to an enzyme preparation

Enzyme production and formulation are linked stages. The resulting preparation has an identity, physical form and specification that need to match the documents used by the customer. Manufacturing-site information is confirmed for the selected product.

Quality information has a defined scope

Cangzhou site documentation

Cangzhou site records include ISO 9001, ISO 22000, FSSC 22000 and FAMI-QS documentation. Each document identifies the organisation, site, certified scope and certificate period for your supplier review.

Tell us the product or manufacturing site and destination market so we can identify the relevant quality documents for your supplier review.

Request the relevant quality documents

Product specifications describe the preparation; a certificate of analysis reports results for a batch; management-system certificates describe the named organisation and certified scope. Include the product, site or batch reference to obtain the relevant record.

Product-specific requirements

For Halal, Kosher or Non-GMO requirements, include the product reference and intended market. We can identify the applicable product information and supporting documents for your review.
