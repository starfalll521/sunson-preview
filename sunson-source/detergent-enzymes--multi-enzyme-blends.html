A blend starts with the cleaning brief

The required enzyme functions should follow the stains and formulation under consideration. A 3-in-1 or 5-in-1 designation identifies the range; it does not by itself establish which substrates a particular blend targets.

Evaluate the blend in your detergent system

Multi Excellase 218 and Multi Excellase 2189 are granular references for review in powder systems. Multi Excellzyme 4283 is the liquid reference for review in a liquid formulation. Check the enzyme composition and activity specification for the selected reference against the target stains.

For liquid detergents, record the proposed addition stage, pH, temperature and exposure time, then plan initial and storage assessments. For powder detergents, review blending, distribution and handling conditions. Keep physical observations with the relevant enzyme-activity measurements.

Stored activity and performance in a wash test answer different questions. Use a defined reference formula, comparable stains and washing conditions, and record any ingredient or process changes. Review each relevant enzyme activity using an appropriate method; a blend designation alone does not establish compatibility or cleaning performance.

Discuss the blend and your formula

For a formulation-specific requirement

For a custom detergent enzyme blend, specify the required functions, activity specification and liquid or granular format. Include the target stains, base formulation, storage or processing conditions and any starting product reference.

Quantity, packaging, destination and any branding needs form part of the supply brief. The proposed specification, development scope and timing are agreed for the project. Territory, exclusivity or brand-use arrangements require separate agreement.
