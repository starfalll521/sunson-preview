The blend designation identifies the product range. Select a product using its technical information and the formulation requirements to be evaluated.

A blend starts with the cleaning brief

The required enzyme functions should follow the stains and formulation under consideration. A 3-in-1 or 5-in-1 designation identifies the range; it does not by itself establish which substrates a particular blend targets.

Liquid and granular options

Multi Excellase 218 and Multi Excellase 2189 are the granular references. Multi Excellzyme 4283 is the liquid reference. The product table keeps their names, forms and reference codes together.
