FORMULATION EVALUATION

Evaluate the blend
in your detergent system.

Check the supplied format, enzyme composition and activity specification for the selected reference against the target stains and detergent system.

Incorporation conditions

Liquid detergents. Record the proposed addition stage, pH, temperature and exposure time.

Powder detergents. Review blending, distribution and handling conditions.

Initial & storage assessments

For liquid detergents, plan initial and storage assessments. Keep physical observations with the relevant enzyme-activity measurements.

Review each relevant enzyme activity using an appropriate method.

Washing performance

Use a defined reference formula, comparable stains and washing conditions, and record any ingredient or process changes.

Stored activity and performance in a wash test answer different questions. A blend designation alone does not establish compatibility or cleaning performance.

CUSTOM FORMULATION

Request a custom blend

For a custom detergent enzyme blend, include the following in your brief.

01 / SPECIFICATION

Functions & format

Specify the required functions, activity specification and liquid or granular format.

02 / APPLICATION

Your formulation

Include the target stains, base formulation, storage or processing conditions and any starting product reference.

03 / SUPPLY

Supply requirements

Quantity, packaging, destination and any branding needs form part of the supply brief.

The proposed specification, development scope and timing are agreed for the project. Territory, exclusivity or brand-use arrangements require separate agreement.
