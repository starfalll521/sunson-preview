Feed enzymes for animal nutrition

Nutrition begins with what animals can use from their feed. Enzyme science offers specific ways to work with feed components. SUNSON connects this biological question with the practical demands of feed production.

From feed ingredients to nutrient use

Phytate phosphorus, dietary protein and the mix of ingredients in poultry diets call for different enzyme functions. These nutritional questions also need to be distinguished from enzyme activity retained during feed manufacturing.

Phytase acts on phytate. Protease acts on dietary protein. A multi-enzyme preparation addresses several feed components; the relevant route depends on the intended animal, diet and use.

Connect the application with the preparation

Once the nutritional task is clear, the product profile adds the intended use and preparation-specific information. SUNSON references include PHY10G phytase, SFQ-081 poultry multi-enzyme and SFP-MUL02 feed protease.
