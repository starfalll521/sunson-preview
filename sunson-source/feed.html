From a nutritional question to an application discussion

SUNSON connects strain research, application development and manufacturing. Its sample process includes technical information and storage and use guidance.

  1. Describe the application

    Include the animal species and stage, diet ingredients and the nutritional or processing outcome you want to measure.

  2. Check the preparation

    Review the product reference and technical information with the team. If a sample is appropriate, confirm the storage and use instructions.

  3. Review the trial feedback

    Keep the enzyme reference, inclusion level and trial conditions with your results. Review retained activity separately from nutritional outcomes.
