Protein digestion in young-animal diets

SFP-MUL02 is used in projects focused on protein digestion in piglets and young poultry. Species, animal stage, protein ingredients and diet composition frame the product-selection question.

Discuss the intended feed use with our team and use the instructions relevant to that diet application. For an upstream protein-hydrolysis process, follow the separate palatant-production profile below.

Protein hydrolysis for palatant production

Palatant-processing document

This SFP-MUL02 sheet covers protein hydrolysis for pet-food palatant production. Its processing examples do not provide complete-feed addition rates.

  • SFP-MUL02 · Palatant-processing sheet, PDF ↗

Palatant manufacturers use SFP-MUL02 to treat protein raw materials before the resulting palatant is incorporated into pet food. The processing product sheet gives the following profile for that use.

Processing characteristicPalatant-production profile
Effective pH range5.0–10.0
Optimum pH range6.0–8.0
Effective temperature range25–60 °C
Optimum temperature range45–60 °C
AppearanceWhite to light yellow solid powder

These ranges describe protein hydrolysis for palatant production. Read the processing sheet below for the original examples; complete-feed addition instructions are separate.
