Phytase for phosphorus utilization in feed

Phytase breaks down phytate in plant-based feed ingredients. When considering PHY10G, start with the animal species and stage, ingredient phytate, and the calcium and phosphorus levels in the diet.

Define the objective before changing the formulation: available phosphorus, inorganic-phosphorus addition and phosphorus excretion are different outcomes. Product activity alone does not establish a phosphorus matrix credit or a mineral replacement rate.

Review matrix evidence for the selected product, dose and diet with the responsible nutrition specialist. Record the reference diet and any formulation changes with the trial results. Claims about lower phosphorus excretion or environmental impact need evidence from the intended application.

Discuss PHY10G in your diet formulation

Thermal performance belongs to the actual process

The thermostable category is relevant when the enzyme encounters feed conditioning and pelleting. Temperature, moisture and residence time determine which thermal evidence is useful; a product name alone does not establish a universal recovery rate.
