Phytate phosphorus and diet formulation

Plant-based feed ingredients contain phytate phosphorus. Phytase hydrolyses phytate, making it relevant to phosphorus use and the formulation of inorganic-phosphorus inputs. The nutritional response is specific to the diet and use conditions.

Phytase and phosphorus utilization

Thermal performance belongs to the actual process

The thermostable category is relevant when the enzyme encounters feed conditioning and pelleting. Temperature, moisture and residence time determine which thermal evidence is useful; a product name alone does not establish a universal recovery rate.
