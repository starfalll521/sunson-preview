Pectinase in your fruit-processing route

In fruit-mash treatment before pressing, the application question concerns the material entering extraction and the amount of juice recovered. Record the fruit condition, mash preparation, addition point, pH, temperature and treatment time, together with the pressing conditions.

Define the input material and collection endpoint before comparing yield. Record water or other additions separately; a clearer juice is not evidence of greater juice recovery.

After extraction, residual pectin, haze and filtration raise a different process question. Confirm that SAD-016 is appropriate for the proposed stage before selecting it; the general clarification guide explains the evaluation, not a model-specific approval.
