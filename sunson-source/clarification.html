Enzymes for juice clarification

Juice clarity depends on the material in suspension and the separation process. Pectinase is relevant to pectin-related treatment; the cause of haze determines whether that is the right starting point.

Identify the barrier to clear juice

Fruit particles, residual pectin and other haze-forming components can raise different processing questions. Locate the problem before selecting an enzyme treatment; not every cloudy juice has the same cause.

Pectin treatment and separation

Pectin-related treatment and removal of suspended material are connected stages. The enzyme product is one part of the process; the separation method and treatment sequence also matter.

Pectinase for juice processing · SAD-016

Clarity, filtration and recovery answer different questions

A clearer liquid is not automatically evidence of a higher juice yield. Keep the clarity objective separate from the amount of recovered juice, and consider filtration as its own process result.

ObjectiveWhat to compare
ClarityThe clarity or haze result of the treated juice against the reference.
FiltrationSeparation or filtration performance under comparable conditions.
Juice recoveryThe recovered juice quantity using the same material and mass-balance boundary.
