Enzyme manufacturing rooted in China

We make enzyme preparations in Yinchuan and Cangzhou. Strain research and application work run alongside production, so what is learned in the laboratory reaches the plant.

Why work with SUNSON?

Research with two connected perspectives

Our strain research center studies enzyme-producing microorganisms. Our application research center investigates how preparations work with materials and process conditions. Microbial strains and gene-sequence submissions to GenBank form part of this research foundation.

See the research foundation

Manufacturing you can get to know

Our manufacturing operations in Yinchuan and Cangzhou include submerged fermentation and membrane concentration. Ask about a site visit or video factory review to understand the production background relevant to your product.

See how we make and test

Application support through sample trials

We provide sample storage and use guidance, technical information and follow-up on trial observations. If a test misses its objective, we review the material, process conditions and product selection before discussing the next step.

Plan a sample evaluation

Sampling, testing and batch documents

We sample and test products after production and provide a certificate of analysis for the relevant batch. For site certification, review the Cangzhou certificates separately and check their named entity, scope and validity period.

View Cangzhou site certificates

Contributing to Chinese enzyme standards

SUNSON has helped draft four Chinese national standards: the enzyme preparation classification guide (2021), protease quality (2023), glucose oxidase assay (2024) and lipase quality (2025). They give the industry a shared way to describe and test an enzyme.

Read about standards participation
