Since 1996, SUNSON has developed enzyme research alongside manufacturing. Strain and application research, production operations and experience in use provide different parts of the work behind an enzyme preparation.

Enzyme manufacturing rooted in China

Our manufacturing operations include Yinchuan and Cangzhou. Our capabilities span strain research, enzyme production and application development. You can discuss the product, its manufacturing background and its intended use with one supplier.

Why work with SUNSON?

Research with two connected perspectives

SUNSON has a strain research center and an application research center. Its research includes gene-sequence submissions to GenBank and a collection of enzyme-producing microbial strains. These provide a foundation for investigating enzymes and their uses.

Explore the research foundation

Manufacturing you can get to know

Manufacturing operations in Yinchuan and Cangzhou include submerged fermentation and membrane concentration. On-site or video factory reviews and relevant production information can be discussed as part of understanding the manufacturing background.

Explore manufacturing and quality

A conversation that continues through testing

Our sample workflow includes storage and use guidance, technical information and follow-up on trial results. When a test misses its objective, the review considers the material, process conditions and product-selection question.

Explore application testing support

Quality information connected to the preparation

The production workflow includes sampling and testing after production and a certificate of analysis. Technical, batch and order-related documents support the relevant product discussion and supply process.

A contribution to shared technical understanding

SUNSON has participated in drafting Chinese national standards covering enzyme classification, quality requirements and activity testing. This is part of its work within the enzyme industry.
