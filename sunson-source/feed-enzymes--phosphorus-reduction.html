Phytase and phosphorus utilization in feed

Phytase acts on phytate in plant-based feed ingredients. Its role connects the phosphorus already present in the diet with decisions about inorganic-phosphorus supplementation.

Make the phosphorus objective explicit

Available phosphorus, inorganic-phosphorus addition and phosphorus excretion are different outcomes. A formulation change should be tied to the outcome it is intended to address, rather than treating all three as interchangeable claims.

Diet composition matters

Ingredient phytate, calcium and phosphorus levels, animal species and stage form the context for phytase use. A matrix value belongs to a defined product and use case.

Thermostable phytase · PHY10G

Review the matrix evidence for the intended application

Request matrix information for the selected product, dose and application. Review the species, diet and conditions represented by the supporting evidence.

Product activity identifies an assay result or specification. It does not by itself establish a phosphorus matrix credit or a replacement quantity for a mineral ingredient.

Have the proposed formulation adjustment reviewed by the responsible nutrition specialist before use.

The environmental connection

Phosphorus management links diet formulation with resource use and excretion. It provides a reason to examine phytase in the feed system; a quantified reduction in emissions or environmental impact still needs evidence for the actual application.

Discuss your phosphorus objective

Describe the animal stage, diet ingredients and the phosphorus formulation question. Identify any matrix or trial information you already use.
