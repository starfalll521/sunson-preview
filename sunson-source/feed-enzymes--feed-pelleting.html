Enzymes for feed pelleting

Feed conditioning and pelleting expose enzymes to heat, moisture and time. For a feed mill, the relevant question is how much useful enzyme activity reaches the finished feed.

The feed mill’s processing question

Conditioning temperature alone does not describe the full process. Residence time, moisture and the location of enzyme addition influence how the product is exposed during manufacture.

Define the sampling points and comparison basis

Agree on what the trial will compare. A measurement before conditioning and one after cooling cover a different process interval from a comparison across the pellet mill alone.

  1. Define the process interval and collect representative samples at its start and end.
  2. Record sample identity, collection point, timing and handling.
  3. Use an appropriate enzyme assay and a comparable reporting basis for both samples.

Review background activity, extraction and moisture-basis differences with the laboratory before interpreting recovery.

Request an assay appropriate for the enzyme and feed matrix. Review background activity, extraction and sample-basis differences with the laboratory before interpreting recovery.

Discuss the sampling and assay requirements

State how activity recovery is calculated

For a defined before-and-after comparison, a possible reporting measure is:

Activity recovery (%) = measured activity after the defined process interval ÷ measured activity before that interval × 100

Use comparable assay and reporting conditions, with a valid baseline greater than zero. State how moisture basis and background activity are handled where they affect the comparison.

The result reflects the selected sampling interval and measurement method. It should not automatically be described as a measure of heat resistance alone.

For a blend, assess the relevant enzyme activities separately. Do not add results from different enzyme assays as a single total activity.

Connect retained activity with the intended diet

A processing result and a nutritional result answer different questions. Retained activity describes the enzyme after manufacture; phosphorus use also depends on the diet and the intended inclusion.
