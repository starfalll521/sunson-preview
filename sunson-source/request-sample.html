From request review to sample evaluation

Define the comparison

Identify the material, selected product or selection question, treatment stage and result you want to measure. Define a reference treatment and the ingredient or process change being tested; keep other relevant conditions comparable.

Our team can review the proposed use and sample requirement. Any additional technical work needs an agreed scope, test responsibilities and reporting arrangements.

Confirm the arrangements

Confirm the sample reference, quantity, storage and use instructions, applicable documents and shipping arrangements before evaluation. Delivery details can be collected once the request is ready to proceed.

Use the relevant product or industry guidance to choose what to record. Feed-processing activity, juice recovery and detergent washing performance require different measurements.

Keep the conditions with the results

Keep sample identity, operating conditions, observations and measured results together. Use comparable methods and units; record the calculation basis and any ingredient or process changes rather than attributing every difference to the enzyme.

Share the original comparison with your feedback. The next discussion can review how the sample was used, whether conditions need adjustment or whether another preparation should be considered.

Questions about sample requests

Can I apply before choosing a product?

Write “Help selecting a product” in the product or application field, then describe the material, intended use and evaluation objective.

Are samples free?

Under SUNSON’s standard sample policy, the sample is free and the customer pays the international courier cost. The product reference, quantity and dispatch arrangements are agreed before shipment.

When will the sample be sent?

Dispatch arrangements will be discussed after the product, availability and delivery requirements have been reviewed.

Can I request more than one reference?

List the references and explain what each would be used to evaluate. Availability and quantities will be reviewed for the request.

Which documents will accompany the sample?

Specify the technical, safety or batch information your team needs. The relevant documents and sharing arrangements will be confirmed for the selected sample.

What if the trial does not meet our objective?

Share the sample reference, trial conditions and reference results. Our team can review the material, how the sample was used and the product-selection question before discussing another evaluation.
