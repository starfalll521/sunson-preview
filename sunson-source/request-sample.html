Questions about sample requests

Can I apply before choosing a product?

Write “Help selecting a product” in the product or application field, then describe the material, intended use and evaluation objective.

Are samples free?

Under SUNSON’s standard sample policy, the sample is free and the customer pays the international courier cost. The product reference, quantity and dispatch arrangements are agreed before shipment.

When will the sample be sent?

Dispatch arrangements will be discussed after the product, availability and delivery requirements have been reviewed.

Can I request more than one reference?

List the references and explain what each would be used to evaluate. Availability and quantities will be reviewed for the request.

Which documents will accompany the sample?

Specify the technical, safety or batch information your team needs. The relevant documents and sharing arrangements will be confirmed for the selected sample.

What if the trial does not meet our objective?

Share the sample reference, trial conditions and reference results. Our team can discuss the product-selection questions and the next evaluation step.
