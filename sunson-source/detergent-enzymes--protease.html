Protease enzymes for detergent formulations

SUNSON’s detergent protease range includes Excellzyme Pro G25XLR in liquid form and Excellase Pro 1125 in granular form. Protease provides the protein-targeting function in the cleaning brief.

An enzyme function linked to the stain

Proteases act on proteins, which connects this category to protein-containing stains. The finished detergent combines that function with the rest of the formulation and the conditions of washing.

Choose the format for the formulation

The range offers a liquid reference for liquid detergent systems and a granular reference for powder systems. Format selection is followed by compatibility, incorporation and storage considerations.

When the brief needs more enzyme functions

A protease and a multi-enzyme blend are different product choices. A blend may be relevant when the formulation brief spans several enzyme targets; the current blend references are listed separately.
