Different documents answer different questions

Technical sheet
Product characteristics and use instructions for the specified grade or application.
SDS or MSDS
Handling and safety information for the product and physical form named in the file.
Site certificate
The company, location and activities covered by that certification.
Batch COA
Reported test results for one identified production batch.
