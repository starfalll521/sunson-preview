Detergent enzymes for formulation

Everyday cleaning depends on the materials in a stain and the formulation around them. Enzymes bring specific biological actions into that system. SUNSON connects those functions with the work of detergent formulation.

Explore the applications
On this page

Begin with the material in the stain

Proteases act on proteins. In detergent applications, this connects the enzyme function with protein-containing stains. The surrounding formulation and washing conditions affect the performance of a particular preparation.

A shared enzyme name does not make preparations interchangeable. Detergent protease information addresses a cleaning application, distinct from protease used in animal nutrition.

Protease for detergent formulations

Combine functions around the cleaning task

Multi-enzyme preparations bring several enzyme functions into a formulation. SUNSON materials include 3-in-1 and 5-in-1 designations; the actual composition must be read in the corresponding product information.

Choose the route around the required functions and detergent system. A blend designation alone does not establish the removal of every stain or performance under every washing condition.

Detergent multi-enzyme blends

Develop around the detergent system

SUNSON’s detergent work includes liquid and granular enzyme references and custom blend discussions. The formulation question connects the required function with the product form and surrounding system.

Our application process includes technical guidance and feedback from sample tests. Incorporation, storage and washing conditions give that feedback context, helping define what to investigate next.
