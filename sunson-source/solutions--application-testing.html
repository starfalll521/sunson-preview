Application testing for your process

SUNSON’s application discussions connect enzyme selection with the material and process in which it will be used. The aim is to resolve a specific production or formulation question.

Explore application support
On this page
Define the processTrial supportReview the findingsYour next step

Bring the material and process into the discussion

Start with the raw material, treatment stage and result you want to change. Include the product reference if known and the operating conditions available for the proposed enzyme-addition step.

Feed processing, juice treatment and detergent formulation call for different information. The corresponding application page helps you identify what to record.

Feed processingJuice clarificationDetergent formulation

Product instructions and practical trial support

Our sample workflow connects product selection with storage and use instructions, technical information and trial feedback. Share the proposed comparison and how you plan to measure the result.

For additional technical work, we agree the work scope, test responsibilities and reporting arrangements with you. This keeps the support matched to the actual project.

Use the feedback for the next decision

After a trial, share the sample reference, observations, measurements and operating conditions. We can review how the product was used and discuss whether to adjust the conditions or consider another preparation.

Keep the original comparison and any process changes with the results so the next step has a clear basis.

Discuss your application

Tell us the material, process stage and objective. You can include a product reference or ask for help identifying the relevant range.
