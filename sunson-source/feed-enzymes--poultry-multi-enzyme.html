SFQ-081 in poultry feed

The SFQ-081 product sheet lists cellulase, xylanase, β-glucanase, protease, amylase, mannanase and pectinase among its enzyme components. These functions address different components of poultry-feed ingredients, including corn and mixed-meal diets.

For poultry feed enzymes, identify the bird type, production stage, main ingredients and nutritional objective. Select trial measures that answer that objective: feed intake, body-weight gain, feed conversion and egg-production measures are different outcomes.

Keep the reference treatment and any changes to ingredients or nutrient specifications visible alongside enzyme addition. Review the results with the nutrition team; the presence of several enzyme activities does not establish a particular production response.

SFQ-081 CCThe SFQ-081 CC reference-grade instructions specify 100 g per ton of complete feed. Match the selected grade and incorporation instructions to the intended feed formulation.

Read the technical sheet before setting the addition rate. Consider the mixing process and any conditioning or pelleting conditions relevant to the feed.

For processing evaluation, define the sampling points and activity-measurement methods. Review reported thermal results against the actual feed matrix and processing conditions.
