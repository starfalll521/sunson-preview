Poultry multi-enzyme blend · SFQ-081

SFQ-081 combines several enzyme activities for poultry feed, including applications in corn and mixed-meal diets. The ingredient mix and production stage define the formulation question.

Match the blend to the poultry diet

The SFQ-081 product sheet lists cellulase, xylanase, β-glucanase, protease, amylase, mannanase and pectinase among its enzyme components. The blend brings several functions into the feed formulation.
